## What I do

**Full-stack development**
- Internal backoffice used daily to manage 2,000+ campaigns and the front-end ad-buying platform (Symfony, Doctrine, Twig)
- Node.js services: ML components, DSP connectors, ingestion pipelines for campaign data
- MCP (Model Context Protocol) APIs connecting LLMs to internal business data

**Infrastructure & DevOps**
- 5 physical and cloud servers plus a fleet of Docker services, including a dedicated vGPU server for AI
- Containerised critical services: near-100% uptime, incidents usually resolved in under 20 minutes
- Monitoring, caching and real-time: Netdata, Redis, OPcache, Mercure

**Applied AI**
- Self-hosted, sovereign LLM stack running in production
- Campaign optimisation across DSPs, and complex DSP reporting turned into readable insight
- Model qualification and benchmarking against concrete business use-cases

**AdTech & AdOps**
- DSP expertise across the major platforms (Adform, DV360, Xandr, PubMatic)
- Pixels and tags, client-side and server-side, and data-integrity validation on any client stack
